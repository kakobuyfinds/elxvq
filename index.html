02 / From discovery to decision

How to use a HooBuy spreadsheet

A product list helps you discover possibilities. Keep the source listing, selected option and purchase service separate in your notes so that you can confirm exactly what you are buying.

STEP 01

Build a shortlist

Choose the product type and open the original catalog record. Record the item identifier, size, color and listed CNY price. A title alone may describe several variants.

STEP 02

Confirm the source

Inspect the seller link provided on the detail page. If you intend to use HooBuy, check whether that exact source listing is currently supported. Do not assume a directory button places an order.

STEP 03

Review the full quote

Confirm options, purchasing fees, inspection arrangements and shipping costs with the service you choose. Save the current return terms before paying.

03 / Better comparisons

Compare fit and evidence.

  • Shoes: compare the seller’s measurements with a pair that already fits. A size label can vary between listings.
  • Hoodies: measure chest width and body length on a garment you own. Check whether the chart uses flat measurements.
  • T-shirts: compare shoulder width, sleeve length and print positioning rather than relying on a fit label.
  • QC photos: distinguish seller images, another buyer’s order and photographs of your own item. Each provides different evidence.
  • Condition: inspect visible stitching, stains, missing pieces and color differences. Photos cannot establish durability or authenticity.

05 / Useful answers

HooBuy spreadsheet FAQ

What is a HooBuy spreadsheet?

A HooBuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links and compare items. This page is an independent web guide with HipobuyFind catalog examples, rather than a downloadable Google Sheet.

Where do the buttons take me?

The “HooBuy spreadsheet” and “Buy it now” buttons open https://hipobuyfind.com. Product source links open the corresponding HipobuyFind detail pages. This site does not take payments or manage orders.

Are these live prices or verified HooBuy listings?

No. These are HipobuyFind catalog prices recorded on November 19, 2026. Availability, options, shipping and HooBuy compatibility have not been verified. Open the current listing before making a decision.

Do product photos prove quality or authenticity?

No. Catalog images are listing images, not QC photos of your own order. Inspection images can help identify visible differences but do not establish authenticity or long-term durability.

What if a product link changes or stops working?

Search the source catalog using the item identifier and compare the replacement page carefully. If the model, options or images differ, do not treat it as the same item. Ask the seller or purchasing service to clarify before paying.

Can I return an item after inspection?

Check the seller’s and purchasing service’s current terms before ordering. Eligibility, deadlines, shipping charges and custom-item exclusions may differ. Do not assume that an inspection automatically provides a refund right.
